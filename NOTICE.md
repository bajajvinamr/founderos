## What FounderOS adds / changes

FounderOS is an opinionated, hosted distribution of the Paperclip agent engine, repackaged and re-themed for solo founders and small operators who want a done-for-you company OS with pre-built department templates, a guided onboarding flow, and a BYO-API-key billing model.

Functional changes vs. upstream Paperclip include (non-exhaustive):

- Rebranded UI, wordmark, favicon, and marketing copy
- New design system (typography, palette, layout shell)
- Pre-built department templates (Chief of Staff, Growth, Content, Finance)
- Onboarding wizard tuned for first-time solo-founder users
- Managed hosting + billing layer (Stripe / Clerk) on top of the open-source engine
